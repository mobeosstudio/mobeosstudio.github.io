Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: your measurements and projects stay on your phone. We do not collect personal data, and the app has no account, no advertising and no tracking.

Who we are

Knitmetry is made by Mobeos. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

What the app stores

Your body measurements, gauge, projects and row counters are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

We do not collect personal data. The app has no account, no advertising and no tracking.

Feedback you choose to send

If you send us feedback about a finished project, you decide what is included. You can send it anonymously. Measurements are never attached without your explicit choice.

Export

When you export a project or a PDF, the file is saved where you choose. Sharing it is up to you.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
